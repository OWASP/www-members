## OWASP Member Benefits

Being an OWASP member helps sustain our mission. You can be a one, two, or lifetime member. Student memberships are available for those currently attending an educational institution. Beyond volunteering opportunities, being an OWASP member has many additional benefits, including:

- Access to partner member benefits (see above!)
- Members and Leaders have access to a Google Workspace for Non Profits account (with far more features than the standard $100 per year GSuite individual account)
- Member and Leader (if applicable) discounts for OWASP Global AppSec conferences
- Early access to videos from OWASP Global AppSec conferences
- Vote in the election (paid members only)
- Become a Committee Officer (paid members only)
- After 12 months continuous paid membership, you can stand for the Board of Directors
- Leaders have access to GitHub Enterprise ($250 per year value)
- Leaders have access to Zoom Workplace for Business and Webinars ($220 per year value)
- Chapter Leaders have access Meetup Pro accounts ($420 per year value)
- Leaders can claim reasonable expenses for their chapter or project (expenses over $250 are subject to pre-approval)

We are in the process of updating our association management software, so Meetup Pro may not be available to all chapters after the transition.

NB: As of 30th September 2024, all leaders must become paid or complimentary members of the OWASP Foundation Inc.
